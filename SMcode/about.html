About StudentMarket

A marketplace built by students, for students.

Our Mission

Make school life more affordable

Textbooks, uniforms, and tutoring add up fast. StudentMarket helps students buy, sell, and share these things directly with each other instead of paying full price every time.

Community First

By students, for students

Everything on this platform is offered by other students — notes from someone who took the class, tutoring from someone who's good at it, uniforms someone's already outgrown.

Simple & Transparent

Know who you're dealing with

Every listing shows who's offering it. Orders are tracked from request to completion, so both sides know exactly where things stand.

How StudentMarket Works

1

Browse

Look through textbooks, notes, tutoring, and uniforms in Services.

2

Choose

Open any listing to see full details, price, and who's offering it.

3

Order

Send a request with a date and message — no back-and-forth needed.

4

Track

Check My Orders anytime to see the status of everything you've requested.
